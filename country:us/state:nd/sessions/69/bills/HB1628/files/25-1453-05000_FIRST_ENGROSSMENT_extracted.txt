20 SECTION 3. Chapter 51-38 of the North Dakota Century Code is created and enacted as
21 follows:
22 51 - 38 - 01. Definitions .
23 As used in this chapter:
24 1. "Kratom" means pure leaf kratom that contains five one - hundredths percent o f
25 7 - hydroxymitragynine or less on a dry weight basis .
26 2. "Kratom extract" means a preparation containing any part of the mitragyna speciosa
27 plant in a concentrated form .
28 3. "Kratom product" means a consumable product containing kratom .
29 4. "Processor" means a person that prepares, manufactures, distributes, wholesales, or
30 stores kratom products.

1 5. "Pure leaf kratom" means the leaf of the mitragyna speciosa plant in fresh,

3 6. "Retailer" means a person that sells or advertises kratom products .
4 51 - 38 - 02. Processor and retailer - License required - Qualifications - Fees .
5 1. A processor or retailer may not prepare, distribute, or sell kratom unless the processor
6 or retailer holds a license issued by the attorney general.
7 2. Qualifications for licensure established by the attorney general must include:
8 a. An applicant, if an individual, must be a legal resident of the United States;
9 b. An applicant, if a corporation, limited liability company, limited partnership,
10 general partnership, or limited liability partnership, must be registered with the
11 secretary of state;
12 c. An applicant or manager may not have been convicted of a felony offense by the
13 federal government or any state, a violation of subdivision d of subsection 1 o f
14 section 19 - 03.1 - 23 or an equivalent offense by the federal government or another
15 state, or, following any conviction of any offense, must be determined to be
16 significantly rehabilitated under section 12.1 - 33 - 02.1 ;
17 d. An applicant's facility or principal place of business must meet local and state
18 sanitation and safety requirements; and
19 e. An applicant for a retailer license may not have a financial interest in a processor
20 business.
21 3. The attorney general may deny the application of an applicant that:
22 a. Engages in fraud or deceit;
23 b. Fails to meet the qualifications for licensure under this chapter or administrative
24 rule; or
25 c. Fails to comply with the requirements under this chapter or administrative rule.
26 4. A separate license is required for each facility or place of business operated or
27 maintained by a processor or retailer.
28 5. An initial application for a processor or retailer license must be accompanied by a fee
29 of ten thousand dollars.
30 6 . The attorney general is not required to review an application that is determined to be
31 incomplete.

19 1. A licensee may not prepare, possess, manufacture, distribute, advertise, or sell a

27 b. Contains kratom extract .
28 c. Imitates the appearance of a candy or confection or is designed or marketed in a
29 manner that appeals to a child.
30 d. Is packaged in a manner likely to deceive an individual of the safe or
31 recommended serving .

22 1. A licensee shall test a kratom product before sale to evaluate the composition of the

25 2. Testing must be conducted by an accredited laboratory approved by the attorney
26 general and documented by the testing laboratory in a certificate of analysis.
27 3. A licensee shall retain the certificate of analysis for each kratom product tested and
28 shall produce the certificate of analysis upon request by the attorney general.
29 4. The licensee shall pay all testing costs.

1 51 - 38 - 06. Attorney general - Enforcement - Authority .
2 1. The attorney general or the attorney general's designee may inspect a licensee's
3 facility or place of business to determine compliance with this chapter.
4 2. A licensee that violates this chapter is subject to seizure of any kratom product tha t
5 does not comply with this chapter or administrative rule and :
6 a. For a first violation, a fine of ten thousand dollars and suspension of the
7 processor's or retailer's license for three days;
8 b. For a second violation, a fine of fifteen thousand dollars and suspension of the
9 processor's or retailer's license for thirty days; and
10 c. For a third violation, a fine of twenty-five thousand dollars and permanent
11 revocation of the processor's or retailer's license.
12 3. A licensee may continue to possess kratom while the processor's or retailer's license
13 is suspended, but may not produce, purchase, sell, or transfer kratom.
14 4. The attorney general shall adopt rules to administer and enforce this chapter. The
15 attorney general may not issue a license under section 51 - 38 - 02 until the rules are
16 adopted.
17 51 - 38 - 07. Penalties .
18 1. It is a class B misdemeanor for any person to prepare, manufacture, distribute,
19 advertise, or sell kratom without a license.
20 2. A licensee that sells a product in violation of chapter 19 - 03.1 may be subject to
21 criminal prosecution and automatic permanent revocation of the processor's or
22 retailer's license.
23 51 - 38 - 08. Kratom regulation operating fund - Attorney general .
24 The kratom regulation operating fund is a special fund in the state treasury administered by
25 the attorney general. The fund consists of all kratom licensing fees collected under this chapter.
26 The attorney general may use moneys in the fund only for enforcement activities in accordance
27 with this chapter, subject to legislative appropriations.
