7 SECTION 4. AMENDMENT. Section 19-03.1-22.3 of the North Dakota Century Code is
8 amended and reenacted as follows:
9 19-03.1-22.3. Ingesting a controlled substance - Venue for violation - Penalty.
10 1. Except as provided in subsectionsubsections 2 and 3, a person who intentionally
11 ingests, inhales, injects, or otherwise takes into the body a controlled substance,
12 unless the substance was obtained directly from a practitioner or pursuant to a valid
13 prescription or order of a practitioner while acting in the course of the practitioner's
14 professional practice, is guilty of a class A misdemeanor. This subsection does not
15 apply to ingesting, inhaling, injecting, or otherwise taking into the body marijuana or
16 tetrahydrocannabinol.
17 2. A person who is under twenty-one years of age and intentionally ingests, inhales,
18 injects, or otherwise takes into the body a controlled substance that is marijuana or
19 tetrahydrocannabinol, unless the substance was medical marijuana obtained in
20 accordance with chapter 19-24.1, is guilty of a class B misdemeanor.
21 3. A person who intentionally ingests, inhales, injects, or otherwise takes into the body a
22 controlled substance that is a kratom alkaloid derivative is guilty of an infraction for a
23 first offense and a class A misdemeanor for a second or subsequent offense.
24 4. The venue for a violation of this section exists in either the jurisdiction in which the
25 controlled substance was ingested, inhaled, injected, or otherwise taken into the body
26 or the jurisdiction in which the controlled substance was detected in the body of the
27 accused.
