1 19-03.1-22.3. Ingesting a controlled substance - Venue for violation - Penalty.
2 1. Except as provided in subsectionsubsections 2 and 3, a person who intentionally
3 ingests, inhales, injects, or otherwise takes into the body a controlled substance,
4 unless the substance was obtained directly from a practitioner or pursuant to a valid
5 prescription or order of a practitioner while acting in the course of the practitioner's
6 professional practice, is guilty of a class A misdemeanor. This subsection does not
7 apply to ingesting, inhaling, injecting, or otherwise taking into the body marijuana or
8 tetrahydrocannabinol.
9 2. A person who is under twenty-one years of age and intentionally ingests, inhales,
10 injects, or otherwise takes into the body a controlled substance that is marijuana or
11 tetrahydrocannabinol, unless the substance was medical marijuana obtained in
12 accordance with chapter 19-24.1, is guilty of a class B misdemeanor.
13 3. A person who intentionally ingests, inhales, injects, or otherwise takes into the body a
14 controlled substance that is a mitragynine synthetic derivative is guilty of an infraction
15 for a first offense and a class A misdemeanor for a second or subsequent offense .
16 4. The venue for a violation of this section exists in either the jurisdiction in which the
17 controlled substance was ingested, inhaled, injected, or otherwise taken into the body
18 or the jurisdiction in which the controlled substance was detected in the body of the
19 accused.

21 Century Code is amended and reenacted as follows:
22 7. a. It is unlawful for any person to willfully, as defined in section 12.1-02-02, possess
23 a controlled substance or a controlled substance analog unless the substance
24 was obtained directly from, or pursuant to, a valid prescription or order of a
25 practitioner while acting in the course of the practitioner's professional practice, or
26 except as otherwise authorized by this chapter, but any person who violates
27 section 12-46-24 or 12-47-21 may not be prosecuted under this subsection.
28 b. Except as otherwise provided in this subsection, any person who violates this
29 subsection is guilty of a class A misdemeanor for the first offense. If a person is
30 convicted of a second or subsequent offense not related to marijuana or,
31 tetrahydrocannabinol, or a mitragynine synthetic derivative under this section or
