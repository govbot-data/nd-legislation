9 SECTION 1. LINE OF CREDIT - BANK OF NORTH DAKOTA - APPROPRIATION -
10 STATE HISTORICAL SOCIETY - NORTH DAKOTA MILITARY MUSEUM PROJECT -
11 DEFICIENCY APPROPRIATION REQUEST - ONE-TIME FUNDING - LEGISLATIVE INTENT -
12 REPORT.
13 1. Subject to the provisions of this section, the state historical society may borrow up to
14 $20,000,000 through a line of credit from the Bank of North Dakota during the period
15 beginning with the effective date of this Act, and ending June 30, 2027, the proceeds
16 of which are appropriated to the state historical society, for the purpose of the North
17 Dakota military museum project for the period beginning with the effective date of this
18 Act, and ending June 30, 2027. The appropriation in this section is considered a
19 one-time funding item.
20 2. The interest rate on the line of credit may not exceed the prevailing interest rate
21 charged to North Dakota governmental entities. If the state historical society accesses
22 the line of credit, the state historical society shall repay the line of credit from other

7 SECTION 2. AMENDMENT. Section 55-01-02.1 of the North Dakota Century Code is
8 amended and reenacted as follows:
9 55-01-02.1. Society to have jurisdiction over heritage center.
10 1. The society has jurisdiction over the administration and operations of the North Dakota
11 heritage center building. The director of the office of management and budget is
12 responsible for maintenance of the heritage center building.
13 2. The society, in consultation with the adjutant general, is responsible for all public
14 displays, signage, and museum content for the North Dakota military gallerymuseum
15 within the North Dakota heritage center building. The society and the adjutant general
16 shall enter a memorandum of agreement to establish policies on public displays,
17 signage, and museum content for the military gallerymuseum. The regimental room of
18 the military gallerymuseum must be named the North Dakota national guard
19 regimental room. The society may name other rooms, displays, galleries, and areas o f
20 the military museum but may not authorize donor naming rights for any part of the
21 military museum without prior approval from the emergency commission and budget
22 section.
23 3. The society shall maintain the collections displayed and stored at the heritage center
24 and shall provide, or arrange, for the security of those collections.
25 4. The society shall establish a policy that authorizes the consumption of alcoholic
26 beverages, including distilled spirits as defined in section 5-01-01, at the heritage
27 center during an event that is open only to invited guests and if the alcoholic
28 beverages are dispensed by a qualified alcoholic beverage licensee.
29 SECTION 3. REPEAL. Subsection 3 of section 6 of chapter 17 of the 2025 Session Laws is
30 repealed.
