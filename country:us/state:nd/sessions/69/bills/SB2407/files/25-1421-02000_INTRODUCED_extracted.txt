1 A BILL for an Act to provide an appropriation to the state historical society for the North Dakota
2 military museum project; to amend and reenact section 55-01-02.1 of the North Dakota Century
3 Code, relating to jurisdiction of the heritage center; to authorize a line of credit; to provide for a
4 report; and to provide an effective date.
5 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF NORTH DAKOTA:
6 SECTION 1. LINE OF CREDIT - BANK OF NORTH DAKOTA - APPROPRIATION -
7 STATE HISTORICAL SOCIETY - NORTH DAKOTA MILITARY MUSEUM PROJECT -
8 DEFICIENCY APPROPRIATION REQUEST - ONE-TIME FUNDING - REPORT.
9 1. Subject to the provisions of this section, the state historical society may borrow up to
10 $35,000,000 through a line of credit from the Bank of North Dakota during the period
11 beginning with the effective date of this Act, and ending June 30, 2027, the proceeds
12 of which are appropriated to the state historical society, for the purpose of the North
13 Dakota military museum project for the period beginning with the effective date of this
14 Act, and ending June 30, 2027. The appropriation in this section is considered a
15 one-time funding item.
16 2. The interest rate on the line of credit may not exceed the prevailing interest rate
17 charged to North Dakota governmental entities. If the state historical society accesses
18 the line of credit, the state historical society shall repay the line of credit from other
19 funds derived from donations appropriated by the sixty-ninth legislative assembly to
20 the state historical society in House Bill No. 1018. If the amount of donations collected
21 through March 31, 2029, is not sufficient to repay the line of credit and accrued
22 interest, the state historical society shall request a deficiency appropriation from the
23 seventy-first legislative assembly for the remaining balance and repay the line of credit
24 by June 30, 2029.

13 SECTION 2. AMENDMENT. Section 55-01-02.1 of the North Dakota Century Code is
14 amended and reenacted as follows:
15 55-01-02.1. Society to have jurisdiction over heritage center.
16 1. The society has jurisdiction over the administration and operations of the North Dakota
17 heritage center building. The director of the office of management and budget is
18 responsible for maintenance of the heritage center building.
19 2. The society, in consultation with the adjutant general, is responsible for all public
20 displays, signage, and museum content for the North Dakota military gallerymuseum
21 within the North Dakota heritage center building. The society and the adjutant general
22 shall enter a memorandum of agreement to establish policies on public displays,
23 signage, and museum content for the military gallerymuseum. The regimental room of
24 the military gallerymuseum must be named the North Dakota national guard
25 regimental room. The society may name other rooms, displays, galleries, and other
26 areas of the military museum but may not authorize donor naming rights for any part of
27 the military museum without prior approval from the emergency commission and
28 budget section.
29 3. The society shall maintain the collections displayed and stored at the heritage center
30 and shall provide, or arrange, for the security of those collections.
